# Compliance Academy

**A multi-agent compliance training experience built on Microsoft Foundry Agent Service.**

You are about to step into the role of lead investigator at *Helix Dynamics*, a fictional biotech that just lost 14 GB of clinical trial data. Five suspects. One perpetrator. Multiple frameworks (SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO 27001) and Helix Dynamics' own policies are in play.

This UI runs alongside a live activity log in a second terminal. Every action you take here triggers real agent orchestration: Foundry IQ retrieval against the compliance knowledge index, streaming responses from Azure OpenAI through the Microsoft Foundry Agent Service, and post-scene compliance grounding from real policy documents.
